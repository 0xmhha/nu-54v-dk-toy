## What these terms cover

These terms are an agreement between the store using the store device app NU54Kiosk (the app) and its publisher, {{publisher.name}}. The app is for testing.

## What the app does and does not do

The app shows the menu, takes orders, passes signatures made by the customer’s phone or board to the store server or the chain, and shows order states and receipts.

The app does not receive a customer’s wallet key or PIN. Whether a payment is final is decided by the chain and the store server.

## What the store takes care of

Let only authorised staff use the device and give its registration and keys to no one. If the device is lost, tell the operator at once so the registration is cut off.

Keep the menu, prices, store name and receipt details correct. The store is responsible for goods and refunds.

Press Delivered only after handing over the goods.

## Payment of the store

With the order contract, the order amount is locked and paid after the customer confirms receipt or, once the deadline has passed, when the store claims it. A dispute ends in payment or refund by decision or deadline. In the older way the store is paid when the payment is processed. These are test assets, so nothing is settled in cash.

## Changes and interruptions

This is a test app, so features can change or stop. Payment can fail while the chain, the server or the network is down.

How and how long in advance the end of the service is announced: {{terms.closing}}.
