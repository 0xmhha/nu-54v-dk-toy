## No cash value

TestUSDC and test network tokens taken by this store device are for testing. They cannot be exchanged for cash or other assets, and no value or refund is promised. A test network can be reset or stopped without notice.

## Keys and backup

A customer’s spending board has no backup. If it is lost or reset, the balance on it cannot be recovered, and protecting the board and its PIN is the customer’s responsibility.

The store is responsible for protecting its keys and the test assets it received.

## Chain records cannot be deleted

Payments, deliveries and refunds stay on a public chain. A final transaction cannot be cancelled or deleted, and neither the app nor the operator can change it.
