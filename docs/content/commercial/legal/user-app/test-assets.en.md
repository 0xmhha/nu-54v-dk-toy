## No cash value

TestUSDC and the other test network coins and tokens in the app are for testing. They cannot be exchanged for cash or other assets, and no value or refund is promised.

Amounts on the screen are test units. A test network can be reset or stopped without notice.

## Do not send real assets

Do not send coins or tokens of a real network to the board account address. Assets the board cannot send cannot be recovered.

## The key is your responsibility

The key of a spending board exists only inside the board and never leaves it. The operator does not have it either. Protecting the board and its PIN is the user’s responsibility.

## There is no backup

A spending board has no recovery phrase and no backup. If the board is lost, breaks or is reset, the balance on it cannot be recovered.

If you forget the PIN, a reset signed by the operator makes the board usable again, but the balance is lost. A return goes only to the address stored beforehand.

Put in only what you will spend.

## If you lose the phone

The order confirmation key is on the phone. If the phone is lost or the app removed, you cannot confirm or dispute those orders, and they are settled by their deadlines.

## Chain records cannot be deleted

Payments and transfers stay on a public chain. A final transaction cannot be cancelled or deleted, and neither the app nor the operator can change it.
