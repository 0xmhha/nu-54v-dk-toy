## What these terms cover

These terms are an agreement between the user of the phone app NU Wallet (the app) and its publisher, {{publisher.name}}.

The app is for testing. It shows the state of a board (a hardware wallet) and helps with store orders, payment and return.

## What the app does and does not do

The app asks the board for signatures and shows the result. The key of a spending board is made inside the board and the app never holds it.

The app does not hold money. In a store that uses the order contract, the contract holds the order amount and pays the store or returns it to the board account after your confirmation or a set deadline. A store that uses the older way is paid when the payment is processed.

Checking the amount and the store on the screen before pressing the board button is up to the user.

## Orders, receipt and disputes

This section applies to stores that use the order contract. Approving a payment locks the order amount in it.

When you press Received in the app after getting the goods, the store is paid and this cannot be undone.

If you did not get the goods or something is wrong, you can open a dispute within the deadline the app shows. After it the store can claim the money.

The store is responsible for the goods, their quality and its service.

## What the user takes care of

Keep the board, its PIN and the phone away from others.

Put only as many test assets on the board as you will spend.

Do not use another person’s board or account without permission, disturb the service, or use the app for unlawful transactions.

## Changes and interruptions

This is a test app, so features can change or stop. Payment and reading can fail while the chain, the store server or the network is down.

How and how long in advance the end of the service is announced: {{terms.closing}}.

## Responsibility

The app cannot undo a loss caused by losing or resetting a board, assets sent to a wrong address, or transactions already final on the chain.

Other limits of responsibility, and the law and court for disputes: {{terms.disputes}}.
