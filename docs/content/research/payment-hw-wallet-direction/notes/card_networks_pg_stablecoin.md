## Visa: what has it launched and how does it work?

### Takeaway
Visa has built out stablecoin rails in three layers. (1) It settles with issuers and acquirers in USDC: $3.5B annualized in November 2025, $7B in April 2026, more than $20B by September 2026, across 9 chains. (2) It runs 160+ "stablecoin-linked card" programs, where the card is ordinary Visa credentials and a program manager (Bridge, Rain, Baanx...) converts the stablecoin to fiat when the card is authorized. (3) It offers tokenization tooling for banks (VTAP). The merchant always receives fiat through normal Visa acceptance. The stablecoin sits only on the cardholder funding side and the issuer settlement side.

### Cited Findings
- 2023: Visa was "one of the first major networks to settle transactions in a stablecoin" and first experimented with USDC settlement in 2021 — [Visa (P)](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.21951.html)
- 2023: Visa expanded stablecoin settlement to Solana and to merchant acquirers Worldpay and Nuvei — [Visa IR 2023 (P)](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2023/Visa-Expands-Stablecoin-Settlement-Capabilities-to-Merchant-Acquirers/default.aspx)
- 2024-10-03: VTAP launched to help banks issue fiat-backed tokens. BBVA tested issuance, transfer and redemption in a sandbox, aiming for a live 2025 pilot on public Ethereum — [Visa IR (P)](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2024/Visa-Introduces-the-Visa-Tokenized-Asset-Platform/default.aspx); [BBVA (P)](https://www.bbva.com/en/innovation/bbva-to-explore-the-potential-of-visas-new-tokenized-asset-platform/)
- 2025-04-30: Visa + Baanx USDC cards. Smart contracts move the stablecoin balance from the consumer's self-custodial wallet to Baanx in real time when the card is authorized, and Baanx converts it to fiat. US first — [CoinDesk (N)](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/04/30/visa-and-baanx-launch-usdc-stablecoin-payment-cards)
- 2025: Visa–Bridge (Stripe) collaboration lets fintechs issue cards that debit a stablecoin balance and pay merchants in fiat. Plans cover 100+ countries. Stripe docs describe "stablecoin-backed cards for Bridge, Privy, or third-party wallets" — [Visa (P)](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.22206.html); [Stripe Docs (P)](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/bridge-stablecoin-cards)
- 2025-12-16: US launch of USDC settlement for US issuers/acquirers on Solana. First banks were Cross River and Lead Bank. Run rate was $3.5B at 2025-11-30. Visa is a design partner of Circle's Arc and plans to run a validator. It launched a Stablecoins Advisory Practice. The release mentions earlier Visa Direct pilots for stablecoin pre-funding and payouts — [Visa (P)](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.21951.html)
- 2026-04-29: Added Arc, Base, Canton, Polygon, Tempo. Supported chains are now Arc, Avalanche, Base, Canton, Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, Stellar, Tempo. Run rate was $7B (+50% QoQ). 130+ stablecoin-linked card programs in 50+ countries — [Visa IR (P)](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-Accelerates-Stablecoin-Momentum-Adding-Five-Blockchains-for-Settlement/default.aspx); [CoinDesk (N)](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/04/29/visa-expands-stablecoin-settlement-network-as-volume-hits-usd7-billion-run-rate)
- 2026-09-08: Settlement run rate above $20B, more than 15x YoY. 160+ stablecoin-linked card programs, with payment volume up almost 200% YoY. Rain (a Visa Principal Member) has financed about $2B of card float through Credit Coop's on-chain revolving credit facility, with zero defaults reported — [The Block (N)](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-09-08-visa-stablecoin-settlement-tops-20-billion-annualized-run-rate-up-more-than-15x-year-over-year-413749)
- The "$20B" is an annualized run rate projected from recent activity. It is not a completed-year total — [KuCoin blog (A)](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/visa-stablecoin-adoption-20-billion-settlements)

### Inferences
- For scale, Visa's total payments volume is in the trillions, so $20B annualized is small. The growth rate, however, shows that issuers such as Rain and Bridge are moving card float onto stablecoins. [Mid]
- Card programs are the dominant UX for consumer stablecoin spending. The user taps or swipes a normal Visa card (physical, or in Apple/Google Pay), and merchants see nothing new. Innovation happens behind the card, in funding and settlement. [High]

### Gaps
- No precise launch date or volume for Visa Direct stablecoin payouts was confirmed in this session. Only "earlier pilots" is sourced.
- I did not verify Visa-specific stablecoin card programs in LatAm, Africa or Asia by name (e.g., Rain/Bridge-powered fintechs in Argentina, Colombia or Mexico). Only aggregate counts ("50+ countries") are sourced.
- The Visa–Bridge press release date (believed to be around 2025-04-30, from memory) is not confirmed in this session.

## Mastercard: Crypto Credential, card programs, settlement, M&A

### Takeaway
Mastercard follows the same pattern as Visa. It supports crypto-wallet-linked cards (MetaMask, OKX, Kraken, Bybit, Gemini, Crypto.com, Binance), merchant and issuer settlement in several regulated stablecoins (live with SoFi on 2026-09-22), Crypto Credential for human-readable addresses, and an 85+ member Crypto Partner Program. It chose to buy BVNK (up to $1.8B) after the Zerohash talks failed.

### Cited Findings
- 2025-04-28: Mastercard announced end-to-end stablecoin capabilities. These include an OKX Card, wallet partners (MetaMask, Kraken, Gemini, Bybit, Crypto.com, Binance, Monavate, Bleap) spending at 150M+ merchant locations, direct merchant stablecoin settlement with Circle, Nuvei and Paxos, and the Crypto Credential rollout, which lets people send assets cross-border to verified usernames instead of raw addresses — [Mastercard (P)](https://www.mastercard.com/news/press/2025/april/mastercard-unveils-end-to-end-capabilities-to-power-stablecoin-transactions-from-wallets-to-checkouts/); [CoinDesk (N)](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/04/28/mastercard-unveils-end-to-end-stablecoin-capabilities-will-launch-card-with-okx)

- The MetaMask card is issued by Baanx on Mastercard (reported since August 2024) — [CoinDesk (N)](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/04/30/visa-and-baanx-launch-usdc-stablecoin-payment-cards)

## Stripe: Bridge, Financial Accounts, Tempo, Privy, acceptance

### Takeaway
Stripe has assembled a full stack. Bridge provides issuance, orchestration and cards. Privy provides embedded wallets. Stablecoin Financial Accounts hold balances for businesses. Tempo is its own L1, and it runs Shopify USDC acceptance. Every one of these layers is custodial or uses embedded MPC-style wallets. None depends on user-held hardware.

### Cited Findings
- 2024-10-21: $1.1B Bridge deal announced. It closed on 2025-02-04 — [Stripe (P)](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-completes-bridge-acquisition); [WhiteSight (A)](https://whitesight.net/stripes-1-1-billion-bridge-to-programmable-money/)
- 2025-05 (Stripe Sessions): Stablecoin Financial Accounts in 101 countries let businesses hold, send and receive USDC and USDB. Stripe also announced AI and stablecoin launches — [Stripe (P)](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/sessions-2025)
- Bridge–Visa cards convert the stablecoin balance to local fiat at the point of sale — [Visa (P)](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.22206.html); [Stripe Docs (P)](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/bridge-stablecoin-cards)
- 2025-06: Privy acquisition, amount undisclosed. Privy's SDK embeds wallets in apps without exposing users to seed phrases — [Bitget News (A)](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560604809611); [WhiteSight (A)](https://whitesight.net/stripes-1-1-billion-bridge-to-programmable-money/)
- Tempo (Stripe + Paradigm): testnet 2025-12-09, mainnet 2026-03-18, alongside the Machine Payments Protocol for AI agents. Fees are paid in stablecoins through a Fee AMM with no native volatile token. Finality is about 0.6s. Klarna plans a stablecoin on Tempo. Stripe, MoneyGram, Flutterwave, OnePay and Coastal Bank settle live on it — [CoinDesk (N)](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/03/18/stripe-led-payments-blockchain-tempo-goes-live-with-protocol-for-ai-agents); [Ledger Insights (N)](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/stripe-paradigm-launch-tempo-blockchain-alongside-machine-payments-standard/); [The Defiant (N)](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/tempo-launches-mainnet-unveils-machine-payments-protocol-with-stripe)
- Tempo testnet partners included Kalshi, Mastercard and UBS — [Yahoo Finance (N)](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/stripe-backed-blockchain-tempo-starts-154904827.html)

### Inferences
- Tempo and Circle's Arc show that big payment firms want to control the settlement chain, including its fees, its finality and whether the chain is compliance-permissioned. They do not want to rely only on general-purpose L1s. [Mid]

### Gaps
- The exact Privy announcement date and price were not confirmed from a primary source.
- Stripe's own stablecoin acceptance launch date and volume were not found apart from the Shopify rollout.

## PayPal PYUSD and Pay with Crypto

### Takeaway
PYUSD, issued by Paxos, is PayPal's settlement asset. "Pay with Crypto" (2025-07-28) lets US merchants accept 100+ coins from external wallets, at 0.99%, and settle in fiat or PYUSD. PayPal performs the conversion, so the merchant never needs to hold crypto.

### Cited Findings
- 2025-07-28: Pay with Crypto lets merchants accept 100+ cryptocurrencies, covering about 90% of crypto market cap. The fee is 0.99%. Merchants settle in fiat or PYUSD, and PYUSD balances earn 4% (per the press release) — [PayPal (P)](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2025-07-28-PayPal-Drives-Crypto-Payments-into-the-Mainstream,-Reducing-Costs-and-Expanding-Global-Commerce)
- PYUSD market cap is about $3.32B, just over 1% of stablecoin supply (search snippet; date unclear) — [CoinGecko (A)](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/paypal-usd)
- PYUSD is accepted as a Mastercard settlement stablecoin — [Mastercard (P)](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/stories/2025/mastercard-stablecoin-utility-and-scale.html)
- PayPal is a member of the Mastercard Crypto Partner Program — [Stablecoin Insider (A)](https://stablecoininsider.org/mastercard-launches-crypto-partner-program/)
- Fiserv agreement to expand stablecoin use — [TheStreet (N)](https://www.thestreet.com/crypto/innovation/paypal-exec-says-pay-with-crypto-feature-lets-millions-spend-digital-assets)

### Gaps

- The market-cap figure is time-unstamped. Check CoinGecko live before quoting it.

## Other PSPs/PGs: Coinbase/Shopify, Circle CPN, Worldpay, Checkout.com, Nuvei, Adyen, Asia, Korea

### Takeaway
Merchant acquirers are adding (a) stablecoin payouts and settlement to merchants, as Worldpay, Nuvei and Checkout.com have done, and (b) checkout acceptance from any wallet, as Shopify/Coinbase/Stripe and PayPal have done. Korea is at the pre-legislation PoC stage: Kakao has worked with Circle and Fireblocks, Toss with Optimism, and KB with KG Inicis and Kaia. No licensed KRW stablecoin payment product is live yet.

## Regulation enabling this (brief)

### Takeaway
The US (GENIUS Act, 2025-07-18), the EU (MiCA stablecoin titles), Hong Kong (Ordinance in force 2025-08-01, first licenses March 2026) and Japan (JPYC under the 2023 Payment Services Act amendments, launched 2025-10-27) now provide licensed-issuer regimes. Card networks cite these as preconditions for settling in "regulated stablecoins". Korea's KRW stablecoin law is still pending.

### Cited Findings
- US GENIUS Act, signed 2025-07-18: only "permitted payment stablecoin issuers" may issue, with 1:1 reserves in high-quality liquid assets (cash, short T-bills). Digital asset service providers may not offer non-permitted stablecoins to US persons. The Act takes effect on the earlier of 18 months after enactment (2027-01-18) or 120 days after final regulations. The OCC issued its NPRM bulletin 2026-3 — [Covington (P)](https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2025/07/the-genius-act-becomes-law-key-provisions-from-the-federal-stablecoin-regulatory-framework); [OCC (P)](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-3.html); [Morgan Lewis 2026-04 (P)](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/04/genius-act-implementation-key-proposals-and-what-comes-next); [KuCoin (A)](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/when-does-the-genius-act-go-into-effect-key-deadlines-for-crypto-users)
- Hong Kong: the Stablecoins Bill passed 2025-05-21 and the Ordinance took effect 2025-08-01, licensing issuers of fiat-referenced stablecoins through the HKMA — [HK Gov (P)](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202506/06/P2025060600275.htm); [HKMA (P)](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/international-financial-centre/stablecoin-issuers/). The first licenses (HSBC; the Standard Chartered/Animoca/HKT JV "Anchorpoint") came in March 2026, with 36 applications assessed — [CoinDesk (N)](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/03/24/hong-kong-awards-first-stablecoin-licenses-to-hsbc-standard-chartered-led-group)
- Japan: JPYC launched the first regulated yen stablecoin on 2025-10-27, backed by deposits and JGBs — [The Block (N)](https://www.theblock.co/post/376199/japan-jpyc-launches-yen-stablecoin)

### Gaps
- EU MiCA: titles III and IV (ARTs/EMTs) have applied since 2024-06-30, and CASP rules since 2024-12-30. These dates come from background knowledge and no source was fetched in this session, so mark them UNVERIFIED and confirm on EUR-Lex/ESMA.
- The final GENIUS Act rules (OCC, Fed, FDIC, Treasury) as of September 2026 were not confirmed. Only proposals are sourced.

## Key observation: custodial vs self-custodial, and the role of hardware wallets

### Takeaway
In almost every product found, the end user's key is custodial or provider-managed. Exchange cards (Crypto.com, Binance, OKX, Bybit, Kraken), fintech cards (Bridge, Rain programs), Stablecoin Financial Accounts, PayPal/PYUSD, and the Korean platform wallets all fall in this group. The main exceptions are cards linked to self-custodial wallets (Baanx/MetaMask Card) and checkout-from-own-wallet flows (Shopify/Coinbase, PayPal Pay with Crypto). Even there, the card is not a signer. A pre-approved smart-contract allowance lets the program manager pull funds when the card is authorized, and fiat conversion is custodial. No product found uses a hardware wallet as the payment instrument.

### Cited Findings
- Baanx/Visa: "smart contracts move a stablecoin balance upon card authorization from the consumer to Baanx in real time". This is described as "self-custodial, real-time" spending — [CoinDesk (N)](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/04/30/visa-and-baanx-launch-usdc-stablecoin-payment-cards)
- Stripe offers cards for "Bridge, Privy, or third-party wallets". Privy wallets avoid seed phrases (embedded, provider-assisted key management) — [Stripe Docs (P)](https://docs.stripe.com/issuing/bridge-stablecoin-cards); [WhiteSight (A)](https://whitesight.net/stripes-1-1-billion-bridge-to-programmable-money/)
- Shopify: shoppers pay "using their preferred crypto wallet" (self-custody possible). Merchants default to fiat and can opt into USDC in their own wallet — [Coinbase (P)](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/coinbase-and-shopify-bring-usdc-payments-on-base-to-millions-of-merchants-worldwide)
- Settlement flows (Visa, Mastercard/SoFi) run between institutions such as issuers, acquirers and banks, and their keys are held institutionally, often through Fireblocks-type MPC custody. For example, Checkout.com settles via Fireblocks and Kakao has an MoU with Fireblocks — [Spark (A)](https://www.spark.money/research/payment-processor-stablecoin-integration); [Coinpaprika (A)](https://coinpaprika.com/news/kakao-pay-kakaobank-sign-stablecoin/)

### Inferences
- UX pattern ranking by adoption: (1) card credential (plastic, Apple/Google Pay NFC) on top of a custodial or allowance-based balance, which dominates; (2) app/QR wallets (Kakao, Toss, Binance Pay, Crypto.com Pay); (3) web checkout from a user wallet (Shopify, PayPal). [High]
- Implication for a hardware-wallet-centered payment product: nothing in the market lets a hardware wallet sign each point-of-sale payment. Card-network latency (authorization in about 1–2s), refunds and chargebacks, and fiat merchant settlement all push programs toward a delegated, pre-authorized model. A realistic design keeps cold keys in the hardware wallet and uses a spending allowance or smart-account session key for the card. The hardware wallet then protects the vault, top-ups and limit changes rather than signing each purchase. [Mid]
- Differentiation opportunity: nearly all incumbents are custodial, so "self-custody + card acceptance" is underserved. The MetaMask/Baanx model is the closest precedent, though it still depends on a licensed program manager (BIN sponsor) and a KYC'd card. [Mid]
- Regulatory risk: under GENIUS and Korean draft rules, issuers and custodial intermediaries carry licensing burdens. A pure self-custody wallet vendor may fall outside custodian licensing but still needs a licensed partner for card issuing and fiat conversion. [Low]

### Gaps
- I found no public data on the share of stablecoin-card volume that comes from self-custodial versus custodial programs.
- No card-network or PG product was found that officially integrates hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor, Tangem) as a payment authenticator. Tangem/Ledger-branded card partnerships may exist, but they were not searched in this session.
